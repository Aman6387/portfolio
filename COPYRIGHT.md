### Recommended GitHub settings (owner)

To add platform-level restrictions on GitHub:

1. **Settings → General → Features** — turn off **Allow forking** (if available for your account/repo).
2. **Settings → General → Pull Requests** — require contributors to follow the [PR template](.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md).
3. When publishing a **Release**, use **Generate release notes** after pasting content from [release_template.md](release_template.md).

These settings do not replace copyright law but reduce casual copying on GitHub.
